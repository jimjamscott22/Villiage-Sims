# Small chicken flock implementation plan

Goal: enjoyable, optional ambient chickens with a small food contribution, following the user's supplied specification.

Architecture: Rust owns a persisted flock, bounded movement and morning baskets; snapshots expose visible animals and baskets. A pure browser counterpart mirrors the rules. Existing catalog, transport, interpolation, atlas and hover paths carry the feature.

Decisions: append a 1×1 shelter costing 15 wood, immediately usable; names Pip (curious), Mabel (sleepy), Poppy (social). Dawn 06:00, dusk 18:00, basket +3 food, cap 3, click cooldown 5 seconds. Newly placed chickens explore for their first minute before early-morning sleep, so a midnight-start village can immediately enjoy them. No feeding or other penalties. Roost at the shelter's walkable perimeter. If construction covers an animal, relocate it to nearby free ground without blocking construction.
